Put your character pictures (avatars) in this folder.

Name each file (or a sub-folder) after the character, plus an optional situation word:

  Hinata/hinata smile.png
  Hinata/hinata_blush.jpg
  sakura_angry.png
  Tsunade - onsen flirty.png

Situation words (English or Indonesian): neutral, happy/senyum, sad/sedih, angry/marah,
surprised/kaget, blush/malu, flirty/genit, love/cinta, serious/serius, hurt/sakit, battle/tarung.
Optional outfit words: onsen, kimono, night, casual.

Then start the game with Play.bat (Windows) or play.sh (Mac/Linux),
or open index.html and use Art Setup -> Import folder.
Anything that isn't recognised can be fixed by hand in Art Setup.
