Put your walking sprite sheets in this folder.

Name each file after the character: $Naruto.png, hinata walk.png, Sakura.png ...
RPG Maker sheets are detected automatically:
  - single character, 3 frames x 4 directions (e.g. 144x192, file name often starts with $)
  - 8-character sheets, 12 x 8 frames (e.g. Actor1.png, 576x384) - choose the character in Art Setup
  - RPG Maker XP (4 x 4) and LPC sheets
  - one image per frame, numbered with a direction word:
      naruto_walk_down_1.png, naruto_walk_down_2.png, naruto_walk_left_1.png ...
    (down/front/depan, up/back/belakang, left/kiri, right/kanan/side)

Characters without a sheet use the game's generated sprites
(also exported as ready-made RPG Maker sheets in ../generated-sprites).
